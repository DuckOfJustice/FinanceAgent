# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Multiple users (the developer/operator and friends/family they've invited), each reviewing their own bank transactions once a month on a shared instance. Each user has their own account, own categories/rules/transactions, and logs in with a username/password; an admin panel lets the operator manage per-user bank-sync configuration.

## Product Purpose

FinanceDuck pulls the user's own bank transactions via Enable Banking, categorizes them via user-defined rules (falling back to "Sonstiges" for anything unmatched), and shows a monthly spending breakdown by category. Success = the user can glance at the dashboard and immediately understand where their money went this month.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, functional. No marketing polish, no marketing chrome - this is a personal tool, not a product being sold. Readability and speed of comprehension over visual flourish.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS analytics dashboard cliches (hero metric tiles, gradient accents, card-grid-everything). This is a one-page personal tool, not a pitch deck.

## Design Principles

- Legibility first: every chart segment must be identifiable without guessing (label + value, not color alone).
- One page, no chrome: no nav, no settings screens, no onboarding - just the data.
- Local-tool honesty: looks like a precise local tool, not a hosted SaaS product.
- Numbers over decoration: amounts and category names carry the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single sighted user, no stated accessibility requirements. Still: don't rely on color alone to distinguish categories (colorblind-safe baseline), keep body text contrast >=4.5:1.
